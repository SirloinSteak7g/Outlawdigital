Rennix Automotive

A premium digital presence for discreet storage of performance, prestige, classic and investment vehicles.

Rennix Automotive
Live projectrennixautomotive.co.uk↗

The brief

Rennix Automotive needed a digital presence as discreet and premium as its vehicle storage service. The site needed to reassure owners that performance, prestige, classic and investment cars would be cared for, while making the storage options and next step easy to understand.

The useful bit

I shaped the site around the questions an owner asks before entrusting a car: where it is stored, what level of care is available and how to start a conversation. Dark cinematic art direction, clear service pages and focused local content give the business a premium feel without making it feel distant.

The result

A calm, credible local presence that helps the right owners find Rennix across Nottinghamshire and take the next step towards a storage enquiry.
