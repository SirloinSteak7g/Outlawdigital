Comfort Workshop / connected platform

One system from first enquiry to a chair in the living room.

A vertical software system purpose-built for a mobility furniture maker: public marketing, lead handling, consultation, quoting, workshop production, delivery and aftercare in one connected product.

01 / Public website

Win the right customers.

The public experience positions premium British-made riser recliners around the person, with free home consultation and white-glove delivery.

  • Collections for traditional, contemporary, pressure-care and everyday support chairs
  • Product, fabric, health-benefit and buyer-guide content
  • SEO landing pages for high-intent searches
  • Trust pages covering consultation, showroom, delivery, VAT relief and made in Britain
  • Brochure requests, home-demo bookings, showroom appointments and live chat
  • Large type, high contrast, click-to-call contact and accessible tap targets
02 / CRM

Turn enquiries into useful conversations.

Every lead carries its source, consent, qualification, estimated value, next action and activity history through the sales pipeline.

  • New enquiry through brochure, demo, quote, follow-up, won, delivered or lost
  • Separate contact and chair-user records
  • Qualification for needs, mobility, money, decision-maker and trade-in
  • Automatic brochure tasks, follow-up reminders and appointment confirmations
  • Live call screens with scripts, missing-information prompts, notes and outcomes
  • Quote PDFs, formal letters and welcome or handover documents
03 / Consultation

Quote on the visit.

The consultation tool supports a 60–90 minute home or showroom fitting without switching between forms, price sheets and separate documents.

  • Needs, goals, chair user, room checks and body measurements
  • Live configuration for range, size, back, arms, cushion, motor and fabric
  • Accessories, delivery options, trade-in and second-chair pricing
  • VAT relief toggle with eligibility explanation
  • Fabric catalogues, quote comparison and customer signature
  • Deposit capture, PDF quote pack and release to production
  • Standalone configurator for instant pricing and stock specification
04 / Workshop, stock and after-sale

Build and deliver without spreadsheets.

Production

  • Person-assigned board from frame and fabric through assembly, sewing and upholstery
  • Promised dates, printable tickets, subtasks and undo
  • Quality-control checklist with finished-chair photo
  • Shared sewing manual with notes and photos per part

Stock and sold chairs

  • Showroom and workshop stock with photos, specification and pricing
  • Sell a stock chair to a lead and connect it to the quote or order
  • Unique chair ID, promised versus actual delivery and payment ledger
  • Stripe balance links, warranty status and welcome letter

Customer completion

  • VAT relief signing and staff countersignature
  • Delivery confirmation with customer signature, photo and notes
  • Stored documents for later reference and aftercare
05 / Roles and KPIs

The right view for every person.

Role-based access keeps the product useful on the shop floor as well as in the office. Admin, consultant, operations and workshop users each get the tools and visibility their work requires.

ExecutiveBusiness health
MarketingSource, consent and campaign performance
PipelineConversion and lost reasons
OperationsProduction and delivery performance
06 / Security

Keep the right information in the right hands.

Staff login, role-based access, audit history and rate limits keep customer and production information organised across the public experience and the internal product.

Google→Enquiry→Consultation→Signed chair→Workshop→Delivery

The result

A high-trust marketing website and a practical internal platform working as one product: better information for customers, fewer handovers for staff and a clearer route from a first conversation to a completed chair.
