Comfort Workshop

The brief

Comfort Workshop needed to explain a deeply personal service without making people work too hard to understand it. The business had expertise in abundance; the digital experience needed to make that expertise feel close, calm and actionable.

The useful bit

We shaped the site around the questions people actually arrive with, then built a guided enquiry path that gathers the right detail without feeling like an assessment. A brand refresh, clearer service architecture and an enquiry system now work as one reassuring first conversation.

The result

An online experience that feels like the start of the service — warm, specific and ready to help.
