### The brief

The Riser Recliner Directory started with a simple question: how do you help someone compare an important, highly personal purchase without adding more noise?

### The useful bit

We designed a structured search experience around the questions people can answer: room, support, fit, budget and need. We then built a content model that makes the information useful to people and discoverable in search.

### The result

A directory that behaves less like a list and more like a helpful guide.
