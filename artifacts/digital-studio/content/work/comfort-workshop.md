### The brief

Comfort Workshop makes British-made riser recliner chairs around the person who will use them. Unlike a standard online purchase, every order carries a considered set of choices — from the consultation and quote to the final specification the workshop uses to build the chair.

The brief was to create more than a brochure website: a connected digital platform for the entire journey. It should help customers find the right product, help the team capture and carry the detail of each order, and give production, delivery and aftercare a clear route from an agreed specification to a finished chair in the living room.

### The public experience

The customer-facing website is built to educate, reassure and generate the right conversations. It brings together the chair collections, fabrics, health benefits, buyer guidance, trust content and clear routes to request a brochure or book a home demonstration.

The experience is designed for older buyers and their families, with large tap targets, strong contrast, click-to-call contact and a calm route through a considered purchase. Every enquiry arrives with useful context for the next conversation.

### One connected platform

Behind the public experience is a business system shaped around a made-to-measure product. It brings together enquiries, customer conversations, consultations, tailored quotes and the operational work that follows, giving the team one shared view from first contact through making, delivery and aftercare.

The platform is designed to support the people doing the work as well as the people buying the chair: less repeated information, clearer handovers and a more dependable route from a first conversation to a finished product.

A high-trust marketing website and a practical internal platform working as one product: better information for customers, fewer handovers for staff and a clearer route from a first conversation to a completed chair.
