### The brief

The Fox at Kirton needed a website with enough warmth and appetite to do the pub justice, while making the practical things — food, rooms and bookings — easy to find.

### The useful bit

We built a flexible content system around the rhythm of the place: menus can change, events can have their moment and a hungry visitor can book without detouring through three other sites.

### The result

One lively digital home for the whole experience.
