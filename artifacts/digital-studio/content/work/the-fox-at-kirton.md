### The brief

The Fox at Kirton needed a website with enough warmth and appetite to do the pub justice, while making the practical things, including food, rooms and bookings, easy to find.

### The useful bit

We built a flexible content system with a private manager login, so the team can update menus, add events and keep opening times, rooms and other important information current without waiting for a developer. Visitors always get the latest version of the pub’s plans.

### The result

One lively digital home for the whole experience.
