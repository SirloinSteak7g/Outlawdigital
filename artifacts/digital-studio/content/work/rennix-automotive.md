### The brief

Rennix Automotive wanted a super quick, responsive website that felt modern and made its vehicle storage service easy to understand. It also needed to rank strongly in local search, bringing more relevant visits without paying for advertising.

### The useful bit

I simplified the journey around the questions a potential customer needs answered, with clear service pages, focused calls to enquire and a structure built for local SEO across Nottinghamshire. The result is a site that loads quickly, works cleanly on mobile and gives searchers a straightforward route to the right information.

### The result

More visibility in local search, a clearer first impression and a better route from visit to enquiry, without relying on paid ads.
