### The brief

When the product range is technical and the decision is personal, a catalogue can quickly become a wall of information. The Specialist Seating Company needed a clearer way to help different audiences find confidence.

### The useful bit

I reorganised the website around needs and outcomes rather than internal product categories. Quote requests now carry the context the team needs, so the first human conversation can start with advice instead of data collection.

### The result

A calmer route through a complex choice, with less admin at both ends.
