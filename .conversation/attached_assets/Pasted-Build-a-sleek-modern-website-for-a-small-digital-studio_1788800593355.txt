Build a sleek, modern website for a small digital studio offering websites, custom apps, CRM systems, business tools, branding, graphic design and local SEO.

The site should feel premium, modern and highly capable, but not like a large corporate agency. It should feel personal, technically strong and design-led.

The main positioning is:

Websites, brands and digital systems built around your business.

The key difference is that the business does more than just build websites. It can also build the systems behind a business, such as CRMs, dashboards, quote tools, booking systems, customer portals, internal apps, digital forms and workflow automation.

The website should show that one person/studio can help with the full digital side of a small business: how it looks, how it works and how customers find it.

DESIGN DIRECTION

The visual style should be:

Very modern
Minimal
Premium
Clean
Strong typography
Lots of whitespace
Subtle animations
Smooth scrolling
High-quality project imagery
Large screenshots
Browser mock-ups
Phone mock-ups
Interactive UI previews
Rounded but not overly playful components
Strong grid layout

Avoid:

Generic agency stock photography
Overly corporate styling
Huge amounts of text
Bright gradients everywhere
Generic AI-looking graphics
Cheap template appearance
Excessive animations

Use actual screenshots and real projects wherever possible.

The website should feel closer to a modern software/product studio than a traditional local web designer.

INTERACTIVE IDEAS

Include subtle interactive features where appropriate:

Animated CRM pipeline

Interactive service cards

Animated mobile/desktop website previews

Keep them elegant and fast rather than gimmicky.

SEO STRUCTURE

Build the site with strong technical SEO from the start.

Use semantic HTML.
Fast performance.
Optimised images.
Proper heading hierarchy.
Schema markup.
Canonical URLs.
Sitemap.
Robots.txt.
Open Graph metadata.
Unique page titles and meta descriptions.

The architecture should also allow future location pages such as:

/web-design-nottingham
/web-design-mansfield
/web-design-newark
/web-design-lincoln
/web-design-derby
/local-seo-nottingham

Do not create hundreds of thin location pages. The structure should simply support genuinely useful location pages later.

OVERALL MESSAGE

The website should make visitors think:

This person can build my website, improve my brand, help me rank locally and also build custom digital systems if my business needs them.

The strongest differentiator is:

Not just websites.

The studio can design and build the entire digital infrastructure around a small business.
