Short portfolio blurb Rennix Automotive needed a site that felt as discreet and premium as their storage service. We designed and built a dark, cinematic brand site that puts the cars and the care front and centre, then structured it for local search across Nottinghamshire and nearby areas — with clear service pages and a conversion-focused enquiry journey.

Longer case study copy Rennix Automotive provides secure, monitored storage for performance, prestige, classic and investment vehicles from a discreet South Nottinghamshire facility. The brief was a premium web presence that matches that positioning: calm, confident, and conversion-led — not a generic garage site.

We created a custom Hugo site with a dark visual system, full-bleed photography, refined typography, and restrained motion (including a light brand intro that never slows the page). Content is organised around how owners actually search: core storage services, specialist guides (classic, supercar, winter/long-term), and dedicated area pages for Nottingham, West Bridgford, Ruddington, Keyworth, Bingham, Loughborough and the wider county. SEO foundations include structured data, canonicals, Open Graph, sitemap/robots, and accessible enquiry forms with spam protection. The result is a fast, mobile-first site that looks premium and is built to generate storage enquiries.
