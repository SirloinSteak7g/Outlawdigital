Elevator pitch (use this first)
Comfort Workshop is a complete sales and factory platform for custom riser recliner chairs. It takes a visitor from Google to a signed chair, through the workshop, and to a signed delivery. Staff get a CRM with phone scripts, a live chair configurator, production boards, stock, VAT relief signing, Stripe pay links, and business KPIs. Customers get an accessible, SEO-ready website with brochure and home-demo booking, live chat, fabric browsing, and trust pages built for older buyers and their families.

What you are selling (one paragraph)
A vertical software system purpose-built for a mobility furniture maker. Public marketing pages generate brochure and demonstration leads. Those leads land in a CRM with a scripted call screen, qualification, and appointment booking. On the visit, staff measure, configure, price (including VAT relief), take a signature and deposit, and release an order. The workshop then builds the chair on a person-assigned production board. After sale, the system tracks payments, warranty, delivery confirmation, and welcome letters. Role-based login separates consultants, workshop, operations, and admin.
