Built around caravan storage. Not adapted to it.
Most storage software starts with a box, a unit and a lock.
Caravan storage works differently.
Vehicles leave and return. A pitch can be empty but still occupied under an active agreement. Different vehicles need different spaces. Customers need access at different times. Staff need to know what is on site, what is away, what is arriving and what needs attention.
This platform is designed around that reality.
Customer → Vehicle → Agreement → Pitch → Movements
One connected record from the first enquiry to the day the vehicle finally leaves storage.
 
⸻
 
The whole site, in one view.
Interactive pitch management
A live digital map gives staff an immediate view of the storage site.
Every pitch can show its current status:
Available Ready for a new customer.
Reserved Allocated to an upcoming arrival.
Occupied A vehicle is currently stored in the space.
Away The customer still rents the pitch, but their caravan or motorhome is temporarily off site.
Unavailable Held back for maintenance, operational reasons or other use.
No more checking spreadsheets against the yard to work out what is actually available.
The benefit
See capacity instantly, avoid double allocations and make better use of every part of the site.

Leaving the site should not end the booking.
Caravan collection and return
This is one of the areas where caravan storage is fundamentally different from ordinary self-storage.
A customer can take their caravan away for a weekend, a holiday or several weeks while continuing to rent exactly the same pitch.
The platform treats that as a movement, not the end of the storage agreement.
Customers can notify the site when they plan to collect their vehicle and when they expect to return.
Staff can immediately see:
On site The vehicle is currently stored.
Collection planned The customer is due to take it away.
Away The vehicle has left but the pitch remains allocated.
Return expected The vehicle is due back.
Returned The vehicle is safely back in storage.
The benefit
Everyone knows what should be on site, what is away and what is expected next.
